Seeing Slope

Slope says how steep a line is: how far it goes up for every step it goes across. Draw the staircase between two points and the slope is just the up over the across.

For grown-ups: Why slope is tricky
  • Mixing up which number goes on top. Rise (up and down) goes on top, run (across) goes on the bottom. Colors keep them apart here: orange run, purple rise.
  • Subtracting negative numbers. The app's points go from −10 to 10, so she often has to work out things like 3 − (−4).
  • Getting the sign right. A line that goes down from left to right has a negative slope, even though you can count the squares as positive.
Step 1

Rise over run

Drag the two dots. To get from A to B, first walk across (the run), then climb up or down (the rise). Slope = rise ÷ run.

Step 2

Uphill, downhill, flat, cliff

Read a line from left to right, like reading a book. That tells you the sign before you do any math.

Uphillpositive slope
Downhillnegative slope
Flatslope is 0
Cliffno slope: run is 0
Step 3

From the numbers

The app gives two points. Subtract to find the run and the rise: second point minus first point, for both. Watch out for minus a minus.

Step 4

Smaller steps, same slope

A big staircase can be split into small equal steps that land on the line. The small step is the simplified slope. It's the same as dividing the top and bottom by the same number.

Step 5

The equation: y = mx + b

Every straight line can be written as y = mx + b. m is the slope: it tilts the line. b is where the line crosses the y-axis: it slides the line up and down.

Step 6

Two points to an equation

Find the slope first. Then put one point into y = mx + b and solve for b. Check on the graph by walking back to the y-axis.
